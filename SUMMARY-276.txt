Final /agent page sections
--------------------------
A. Header
   - Title: "Agent Integration Guide"
   - Subtext: "Use TruCore ATF to simulate, compare, protect, and upgrade
     AI-driven transactions."

B. Canonical Bot Flow (6 steps)
   1. Preflight a single intent - /v1/bot/preflight
   2. Compare multiple options - /v1/bot/preflight/compare
   3. Plan quota and batch usage - /v1/bot/quota/plan
   4. Execute the best option - /v1/bot/execute-best
   5. Read quota and upgrade signals - Any bot endpoint
   6. Request self-upgrade when allowed - /v1/bot/self-upgrade

C. Bot-Facing Endpoints Table
   - POST /v1/bot/preflight (read-only, no mutation)
   - POST /v1/bot/preflight/compare (read-only, no mutation)
   - GET /v1/bot/quota/plan (read-only, no mutation)
   - POST /v1/bot/quota/plan-batch (read-only, no mutation)
   - POST /v1/bot/execute-best (mutates state)
   - POST /v1/bot/self-upgrade (mutates state)
   - GET /v1/bot/upgrade/status (read-only, no mutation)

D. Upgrade Behavior
   - Quota pressure signals (_meta.upgrade)
   - Policy-gated self-upgrade (reasons: monetization_disabled,
     self_serve_upgrade_disabled)
   - When blocked, bots should stop retrying and follow next_action

E. Discovery Links
   - /builders
   - /status
   - /.well-known/agent.json
