# Testimonial Candidates

> Identify users who could become public advocates for ATF.
> Every entry must be a real person from a real interaction.

---

## What Makes a Good Testimonial Candidate

A user who:

- Understood ATF quickly without hand-holding
- Asked deeper questions (integration, architecture, edge cases)
- Mentioned a specific use case for their own system
- Reacted positively to the receipt or verification flow
- Returned for a second interaction or follow-up
- Offered to share feedback publicly

---

## Candidate Qualification Tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Ask |
| ---- | -------- | --- |
| T1 - Ready | Positive reaction + integration mention + willing to be public | Written testimonial or tweet |
| T2 - Warm | Positive reaction + tried CLI + engaged in conversation | Permission to quote a specific statement |
| T3 - Early | Showed interest + asked questions, hasn't tried yet | Keep nurturing, don't ask for testimonial yet |

## Follow-up Templates

### For T1 Candidates (Ready)

> Hey [name], thanks for trying ATF. Your feedback has been really helpful. Would you be open to a short quote we could share? Even one line about your experience would mean a lot.

### For T2 Candidates (Warm)

> Thanks for checking out ATF, [name]. You mentioned [specific thing they said]. Would it be okay if we referenced that feedback? Happy to keep it anonymous if you prefer.

### For T3 Candidates (Early)

> No ask yet. Keep sharing relevant content, answer their questions, and wait for a stronger signal before requesting anything.

---

## Testimonial Output Formats

Once a candidate agrees, capture their testimonial in one of these formats:

### Short Quote (1 line)

For landing page, README, or tweet.

```text
"[exact quote]" — [name/handle], [role]
```

### Medium Quote (2-3 lines)

For blog post or case study teaser.

```text
"[quote line 1]
[quote line 2]"
— [name/handle], [role], [company/project if applicable]
```

### Full Testimonial (paragraph)

For case study or pitch deck.

```text
[name/handle] · [role] · [company/project]
[date]

[full paragraph in their words]
```

---

## Rules

- **Never add someone as a candidate based on assumptions.** Every entry must come from a real interaction.
- **Never fabricate a tier.** If someone only liked a tweet, they are not T2.
- **Never ask T3 candidates for testimonials.** Let them reach T2 naturally.
- **Always get explicit permission** before using anyone's name or words publicly.
